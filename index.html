ABOUT ME

From Aviation to AI — My Creative Journey

My background originated in the high-precision world of aviation and international travel consultancy. Holding a Bachelor's Degree from the University of Calicut alongside an IATA-certified Diploma in Aviation & Airport Management, I developed a meticulous eye for structure, cross-cultural communication, and logistical excellence.

Driven by an undeniable passion for visual storytelling, I made a pivotal transition into digital media. By mastering generative algorithms, ComfyUI pipelines, and 3D motion design, I now transform complex ideas into cinematic AI videos and high-impact AI-generated content.

Today, I collaborate with global brands, creative agencies, and forward-looking studios to craft visuals that break traditional boundaries—merging analytical precision with artistic direction.
